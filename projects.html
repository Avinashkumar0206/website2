Flagship Residential Project - Avinash Elite Apartments

Located in the heart of Anna Nagar, Avinash Elite is a G+5 gated community of 32 well-planned two and three bedroom apartments. The project features a rooftop clubhouse, a landscaped courtyard, 100% power backup, rainwater harvesting, and a fully automated intercom system. Our construction experts used M30 grade concrete for all structural members and applied twin-layer waterproofing on every terrace - a level of finish that reinforced our reputation as the best construction company in Madurai. All 32 units were sold before completion.

Signature Villa - Avinash Palm Villa

Palm Villa is a bespoke 4 BHK duplex in KK Nagar, designed for a returning NRI family. The brief was ambitious: a home that combined South Indian character with contemporary lines, sustainable features, and complete privacy. Our team delivered on all counts - reclaimed teak entrance doors, Italian marble living room, home automation, a small indoor courtyard and a rooftop yoga deck. The villa has since been featured in a leading regional architecture magazine as one of the best construction projects of 2024.

Commercial Landmark - Avinash Corporate Tower

Located on Bypass Road, Corporate Tower is a G+7 commercial building comprising ground-floor retail, six upper floors of office space and a rooftop cafe. The project posed unique challenges - a narrow plot, heavy pedestrian footfall during construction, and a strict 22-month deadline. Our best building professionals deployed advanced tower cranes and a night-shift schedule to meet the deadline without disturbing neighbouring businesses. The building is now 95% occupied and remains one of the most visible symbols of our capability.
